Where we pick up

Free computer pickup and recycling for offices in Philadelphia, on the Main Line and in Delaware County. These are the towns and neighborhoods we cover.

Philadelphia

Every neighborhood, including Center City, Old City, University City, Fishtown and Northern Liberties, South Philadelphia, Manayunk, Roxborough and East Falls, Chestnut Hill, Mount Airy and Germantown, and the Northeast.

The Main Line and nearby

Lower Merion Township: Ardmore, Bala Cynwyd, Bryn Mawr, Gladwyne, Haverford, Merion, Narberth and Wynnewood. Radnor Township: Wayne, Radnor, Rosemont, St. Davids and Villanova. Conshohocken and West Conshohocken.

Delaware County

Havertown and Haverford Township, Upper Darby, Drexel Hill, Broomall, Newtown Square, Springfield, Media, Swarthmore and Wallingford.

What every pickup includes

Pickup is free for three or more laptops, desktops or all-in-ones, working or not. Every hard drive is shredded by Elemental, an R2v3-certified recycler in Philadelphia, and you get a certificate listing each one by serial number. Newer machines can get a cash offer. How pickup works.
