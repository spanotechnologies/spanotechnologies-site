What's next

In the works.

What we're building toward. These are plans, not promises, and dates get added when they are firm.

  1. Live now

    Business laptop pickup

    Offices in Philadelphia, the Main Line and Delaware County retiring three or more laptops or desktops can get a free pickup. Drives are destroyed on site, and a certificate lists every serial number. How it works.

  2. Live now

    Current stock on this site

    The home page shows what is listed in the eBay store, next to what has recently sold. It updates itself from eBay every few hours.

  3. In progress

    More stock, more often

    We're lining up larger, more regular batches of business laptops, so there is less waiting between drops and more choice in each one.

  4. Planned

    Ready-to-run laptops

    Most of our laptops are sold barebones today. We plan to offer units with a drive installed, Windows set up and a charger in the box, for buyers who want to open it and start working.

  5. Exploring

    Stock alerts

    An email when a new batch goes up, so regulars see it first. Until then, saving the store on eBay does the same job.
