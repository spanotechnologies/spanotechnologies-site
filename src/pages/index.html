How it works

  1. Send a list

    How many, and the makes and models if you have them. Newer machines get a per-unit offer in writing before we come.

  2. We pick up

    Each machine is logged by serial number on a transfer form you sign, and we take everything with us.

  3. You get a certificate

    Every drive is shredded by a certified recycler and listed by serial number on your certificate. See a sample.

Why it's free

We refurbish and resell the machines that still work, and that pays for the pickup. Asset tags come off first, and your company's name never goes with them. Whatever can't be reused is recycled, not landfilled.

It suits small offices: accounting and law firms, insurance agencies, design studios and nonprofits retiring three or more laptops, desktops or all-in-ones, working or not. Phones, tablets, monitors and docks can come along too.
