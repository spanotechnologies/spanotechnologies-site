How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

  1. Tell us what you have

    Email a rough count and the makes and models. We reply with a pickup time and, for newer machines, a per-unit offer in writing before we come.

  2. We pick up

    We log each machine by serial number on a signed transfer form and take everything with us, so the visit is quick. Your equipment stays locked up until its drives are shredded.

  3. You get the paperwork

    A signed transfer of ownership at pickup, then a certificate of data destruction listing every device by serial number. See a sample.

What you can count on

Every drive accounted for.

Shredded, with proof

Each drive is removed, its serial number recorded, and kept locked until an R2v3-certified recycler shreds it. Your certificate carries the recycler's shredding receipt.

Nothing that identifies you

Company asset tags and stickers come off before a machine is resold, and your company's name never appears in a listing.

Mac storage erased or shredded

Built-in storage, like a MacBook's, is erased with Apple's own erase function before reuse, or shredded if your policy requires it. Whatever can't be reused is recycled at no charge.

Why it's free

We refurbish what can be reused.

Working machines are tested, cleaned and resold with no trace of your company on them. That resale is what pays for the pickup, and it's why newer business laptops and desktops can get a cash offer.
