About

A small local business. Every machine handled by hand.

Spano Technologies is run by Cole Spano in Philadelphia. We pick up retired computers from small offices, make sure every drive is shredded, and give the machines that still work a second life.

Why reuse

Business computers have a longer life than the one they've had.

Built to last

ThinkPads, Latitudes, OptiPlexes and their peers are designed for years of daily work, with sturdier parts than most consumer machines.

Retired on a schedule

Offices replace them on a refresh cycle, not because they're worn out. Most have years of use left.

Recycled when they're done

What can't be reused goes to an R2v3-certified electronics recycler, not a landfill.

What we do

Offices in Philadelphia, the Main Line and Delaware County call us when they retire three or more laptops or desktops. We pick them up for free, have every drive shredded by an R2v3-certified recycler, and send a certificate listing each drive by serial number with the recycler's receipt. How pickup works.

How a machine is checked

Each one is powered on and checked: the BIOS, battery, screen, keyboard and ports. Its specs come from the machine itself, and its condition is written plainly. A machine that doesn't work is sold for parts and labeled that way.
