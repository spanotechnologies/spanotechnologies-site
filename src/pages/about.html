Why reuse them

Business laptops and desktops are built for years of daily use, and offices usually replace them on a schedule, not because they've worn out. Most have years left. The ones that don't are recycled through an R2v3-certified electronics recycler, not sent to a landfill.

How a machine is checked

Each one is powered on and checked: the BIOS, battery, screen, keyboard and ports. Its specs come from the machine itself, and the listing says what works and what doesn't. A machine that doesn't fully work is sold for parts and labeled that way. Before anything is listed, its drive has been shredded or erased and every asset tag is off.
