What we do

We resell used electronics on eBay. Most of what we sell is business laptops and computer memory, alongside iPhones, iPads and other electronics as they come in. We test, photograph, list and ship everything ourselves.

How a laptop gets listed

Each machine is powered on and checked before it goes up: the BIOS, battery, screen, keyboard and ports. The specs in the listing come from the machine itself, not from a guess at the model.

The condition is written plainly. If something is wrong, it is in the description and, for a bigger fault, in the title. A machine that doesn't work is sold for parts and labeled that way.

Memory

RAM is listed from the label on the stick: type, capacity, speed and module type. Matched kits are listed and sold as kits.

iPhone and iPad

Every iPhone and iPad is checked for Activation Lock, so it sets up under your own Apple ID. We check the carrier lock and the IMEI, and the listing gives the battery health percentage.
