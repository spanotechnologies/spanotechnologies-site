How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

  1. Tell us what you have

    Email a rough list: how many, what make and model, and about how old. We reply with a pickup time and, for newer machines, a per-unit offer in writing before we come.

  2. We pick up

    Each drive is pulled and destroyed in front of you before anything leaves your office. Built-in storage, like a MacBook's, is erased on site, unless your policy calls for physical destruction.

  3. You get the paperwork

    A signed transfer of ownership at pickup, then a certificate of data destruction listing every device by serial number within 10 business days. See a sample.

What we take

Built for small offices.

Three or more

Laptops and desktops, any brand. Three devices is the minimum for a free pickup.

Working or not

Old, slow and broken machines are fine. Anything that can't be reused is recycled through a certified electronics recycler, not sent to a landfill.

Cash for newer machines

Business laptops and desktops from roughly the last five years, and Apple silicon Macs, can get a per-unit offer. Older machines are a free pickup.

Before we come

Four things to do first.

Locked machines can't be reused, so these make the biggest difference to what we can offer.

Back up your data

Copy anything you want to keep. Once a drive is destroyed, nothing on it can be returned.

Release device management

Remove the machines from Windows Autopilot and Intune, Apple Business Manager, and Absolute, or whatever tool your IT provider uses.

Remove BIOS passwords

Or tell us the password, so the machine can be reset and reused.

Gather the chargers

If you still have them. They help a machine find its next owner.

Questions

Where do you pick up?

Philadelphia, the Main Line and Delaware County, including Lower Merion, Radnor and Haverford Townships and Conshohocken. Pickups are early mornings, lunchtime, late afternoons, or Saturdays if your office is open.

Is it really free?

Yes, for three or more devices in our area. There is nothing to pay, including for recycling what can't be reused.

Can I watch the drives being destroyed?

Yes, and we'd rather you did. Each drive is pulled, its serial number written down, and the drive destroyed and photographed before we leave.

What about MacBooks and built-in storage?

Storage that can't be removed is erased on site with the maker's own erase function, so the machine can be reused. If your policy requires physical destruction or a recycling receipt, tell us before the pickup. Those devices go to a certified recycler for shredding, and the receipt is listed on your certificate.

How does payment work?

For machines that qualify, we email a per-unit offer before the pickup and pay it at the pickup. The amount is written on the transfer of ownership.

Are you a certified ITAD company?

No. We're a small business, not a certified disposal facility. If your policy requires a certified vendor, PCs for People, the nonprofit partner in the city's PHLDonateTech program, offers free business pickup for 15 or more usable computers.

Do you take equipment from healthcare offices?

Not yet. Medical, dental and other offices holding patient records need a different agreement, and we don't offer one today.

What happens to the machines?

Working machines are tested and resold through our eBay store. Some that don't fully work are sold there as parts units, labeled that way. None carries any data from your company. The rest is recycled through a certified electronics recycler.

Tell us what you have.

A rough count and the makes and models are enough to start. We reply by email.
