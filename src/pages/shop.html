Buying from us

Does a laptop come with a charger, a drive or Windows?

Usually not. Most of our business laptops are sold barebones: no storage drive, no operating system and no charger. The “What's included” line in each listing says exactly what ships. See the buying guide.

Will it run Windows 11?

Laptops with an 8th-generation Intel processor or newer generally support Windows 11. Each listing gives the exact processor so you can check.

What does the battery note mean?

“Holds charge” means it ran on battery during testing. “AC only” means it works on the charger but the battery does not hold. “Not tested” means we could not check it. We don't quote cycle counts.

Is the laptop locked?

We check each machine for BIOS passwords and theft-tracking locks before listing. If we find one, the listing says so.

How fast do you ship?

Within one business day of the order, with tracking through eBay.

What is the return policy?

Used items have 30-day returns through eBay. Items sold for parts are as-is and cannot be returned.

Can I make an offer?

Most listings accept Best Offer on eBay. Send one from the listing page.

Can I buy directly, outside eBay?

No. Every sale goes through eBay, so your purchase is covered by eBay's Money Back Guarantee.

Where do the machines come from?

Businesses retiring their equipment. Drives are removed or erased before anything is listed, and nothing that identifies the company a machine came from appears in a listing.
