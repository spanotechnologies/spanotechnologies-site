Privacy

Privacy policy.

The short version: we collect only what you send us, we use it to arrange your pickup, and we never sell it.

Last updated November 23, 2026. Spano Technologies LLC, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

What we collect

When you fill in the pickup request form, email or call us, we receive what you choose to send: your business name, your name, email address, phone number, pickup address, the devices you have, the times that suit you, any notes, and how you heard about us.

This site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Its fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

How we use it

To reply to you, schedule the pickup, send any offer for your machines, and prepare the paperwork: the signed transfer of ownership and the certificate of data destruction. We may contact you later about that same pickup, for example to send the certificate. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or rent your information.

Who else sees it

Only the services that carry it to us, and only as far as needed:

  • Web3Forms delivers the request form to our email.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. We don't receive or use them.

Our certified recycler receives drives and drive serial numbers, never your business's name. We'd share information beyond this only if the law required it.

The data on your computers

We don't open, copy or review files on the drives we collect. A machine is powered on only to its firmware screens, to read serial numbers and lock status, until its storage has been removed or erased. Every step from pickup to shredding is set out in our Data Destruction and Chain-of-Custody Policy.

How long we keep it

For a completed pickup, the signed transfer, our pickup log, the certificate and the recycler's receipts are kept for at least three years, so we can answer questions about them later. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except for those records.

eBay buyers

If you bought from our eBay store, eBay's own privacy notice covers your order. We use the name and address eBay gives us only to ship that order and help with it.

Questions or requests

Email info@spanotechnologies.com or call (484) 957-1712 to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
