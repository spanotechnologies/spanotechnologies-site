About

A small shop. Every unit checked by hand.

Spano Technologies is a small business run by Cole Spano. We have sold on eBay since 2014: more than 600 items, with 100% positive feedback. We test, photograph, list and ship everything ourselves.

What we do

We resell used business laptops and computer memory on eBay. Business machines like ThinkPads and Latitudes are built to last, and most of them have years of use left in them.

How a laptop gets listed

Each machine is powered on and checked before it goes up: the BIOS, battery, screen, keyboard and ports. The specs in the listing come from the machine itself, not from a guess at the model.

The condition is written plainly. If something is wrong, it is in the description and, for a bigger fault, in the title. A machine that doesn't work is sold for parts and labeled that way.

Memory

RAM is listed from the label on the stick: type, capacity, speed and module type. Matched kits are listed and sold as kits.

Buying from us

  • Orders ship within one business day.
  • Used items come with 30-day returns through eBay.
  • Parts units are sold as-is.
